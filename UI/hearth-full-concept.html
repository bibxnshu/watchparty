A watch party, built for exactly two

Sit close, press play, watch it together — from anywhere.

Hearth streams your own local file straight to the person you're watching with. No uploads, no accounts for them to fumble with, no sync to fight — just a private room that feels like sharing a couch.

Everything for movie night

Nothing extra to set up

Hearth keeps the whole experience down to a room, a stream, and a chat — no libraries, no shared drives, no fuss.

🎞️

Stream your own copy

The host plays a local file — Hearth captures the video output and streams it live, so nobody has to upload or own a copy.

🔁

Perfectly in sync

Because the viewer is watching a live feed, pausing, rewinding, or seeking happens for both of you at once. No sync logic to break.

💬

Chat next to the screen

A text channel rides alongside the stream over an encrypted peer-to-peer data channel — no server ever sees your messages.

👋

One tap to join

See when your person is hosting right from your friends list, and join their room with a single tap — no code needed.

How it works

Three steps to lights-down

01

02

03

Press play, together

The viewer sees exactly what the host sees, the moment it happens — curtains up.
